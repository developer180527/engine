test: AnimatorSystem behavior — incl. the kMaxBones stack-smash guard

The animation MATH was covered (anim_pose_test, clip_binding_test) but the
ECS system driving it had no test at all. Closes the gap ENGINE_STATUS.md
flagged as the cheapest real coverage win.

The reason this file exists is the `boneCount > kMaxBones` guard, which is a
silent-corruption path: computeBindPosePalette declares
`float worldMatrices[kMaxBones * 16]` on the stack and SkinnedMesh carries a
fixed skinMatrices[128 * 16]. A 129-bone skeleton slipping past the check
smashes both, with no error reported. So the asserted contract is not "it
returns false" — it is "an oversized skeleton NEVER writes a palette", on
BOTH routes into the writer (clip sampling and the no-clip bind-pose path).

Proven to fail: deleting the two guards aborts the process via the stack
protector (SIGABRT, exit 134) after the first assertion. Restored
byte-identical afterwards.

Also covered: the 128-bone boundary (the guard is `>`, not `>=` — an
off-by-one would silently kill skinning on max-size rigs), bind-pose fallback
with no clip, cleared palette flag on a missing skeleton (not left stale),
time += dt*speed, paused animators frozen, looping fmod wrap, non-looping
clamp-and-auto-stop, and both reverse-playback paths — reverse loop matters
because fmod(-0.2, 1.0) is negative in C, so the duration must be added back
or ozz gets a negative ratio and the pose silently stops updating.

Hermetic: skeletons and clips are built straight through ozz's offline
builders, so no Assimp, no asset files, no GPU — it belongs in the `unit`
lane and gates CI, unlike anim_pose_test/clip_binding_test.
