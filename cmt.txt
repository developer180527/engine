assets: fix silently untextured shipped builds; sibling .ctex never resolved

A cooked mesh's embedded textures are extra outputs written BESIDE the cooked
file, with the material storing only a basename — src/assets/info.md states the
rule: "the loader resolves that against the cooked file's own directory". Both
resolveTexture (sync) and resolveTextureGPU (worker) ignored it and resolved
exclusively through AssetRegistry::findBySourcePath. Siblings are not registry
assets, so that lookup could never succeed.

The failure was silent and looked like a success: every material resolved to no
texture, the game rendered untextured, and the resulting "textures 0.0 MB" was
reported (by me) as a BC-compression win rather than a bug. Only counting live
handles — 6, exactly the built-ins — exposed it.

Both paths now try the direct sibling file first and fall back to the registry
for genuine source-asset references. The sync path routes through
loadTextureFromCooked, which is cache-backed, so materials naming the same
sibling share one upload.

Measured, shipped fps_shooter: textures 6 -> 11 live, 0.0 -> 40.7 MB. The
pistol's 4 material references produced 2 uploads, confirming the per-mesh
staging dedup works. Budget at low tier: 63.7 MB total (over on render
targets), and 51.7 MB — PASS — with shadowResolution 1024.

25/25 unit, 2/2 fuzz-regress.
