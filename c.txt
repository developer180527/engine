render: implement R7 material-bind dedup, and measure it at zero

R7 said materialBinds == draws means no dedup is happening. Implemented, and measured
at EXACTLY zero benefit on real content. The negative result is the valuable part, so
it is recorded rather than quietly shipped as a win.

  scene                      draws                      material binds
  5 000 real MegaKit props   2 869 (2 811 submesh)      2 869 , not one hit
  20 000 cubes               1                          1

WHY, AND WHY A DEEPER CACHE WOULD BE A BUG. The dedup can only skip a material's
uniform uploads when that material was bound by the IMMEDIATELY PREVIOUS draw: bgfx
holds one set of uniform values at a time, so skipping an upload because the material
was bound two draws ago would render it with the other material's values. One-deep is
the only correct depth.

One-deep cannot hit here because SUBMESHES ARE EXPANDED AT SUBMIT TIME, outside the
visible set and outside the sort. The sort groups draws by the ITEM's material key,
then each item loops its ranges binding a different material per range, so the bind
sequence is A, B, C, A, B, C… With ~1.7 material groups per mesh that is every
multi-material prop in the kit, and the single-material items that WOULD group are
exactly the ones that instance instead and never reach the per-draw path. Zero hits is
what the structure predicts, not a bug in the dedup.

THE PREREQUISITE IS THE SAME ONE BLOCKING INSTANCING. A visible set with one entry per
item cannot order submesh draws, so they can neither batch nor dedup, which is also
why 50 000 real props produced ZERO instanced submits. Both open findings are one root
cause: submesh draws are second-class citizens of the sort. Fixing it means VisibleDraw
addressing an (item, submesh) pair so the key can carry the range's own material, at the
cost of a larger visible set and a sort over more entries. Worth starting from the
measurement that 98% of draws in this scene (2 811 of 2 869) are submesh draws.

KEPT ANYWAY, AND NOT FOR SPEED. No performance claim is made. The change split the
material's UNIFORM uploads from bindDrawState's per-draw encoder state, and that
distinction was implicit and easy to get wrong: bgfx PERSISTS uniform values across
submits but DISCARDS textures, state, transform and vertex buffer at every submit. That
is now written down at the code. It also makes materialBinds count REAL binds rather
than draws, so when submesh sorting lands the counter will show the win instead of
needing a new metric.

Verified the shadow pass never touches m_uParams/m_uColorFactor/m_uTexFlags, so the
cache cannot be invalidated across passes, and it resets at the top of every view. The
data-driven path is deliberately NOT deduped, its uniform blocks and sampler set are
variable-length, so caching them means caching a vector and invalidating it correctly,
and nothing has measured it as a cost.

52/52 ctest. Cube-scene submit counters unchanged. Recorded as issues.md R17.
